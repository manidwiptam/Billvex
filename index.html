Privacy & Data Security

100% Local-First & Private:

  • All invoice data, customer details, tax figures, bank accounts, and uploaded logos are processed purely inside your local browser.
  • No invoice data is sent to external servers or cloud databases.
  • No telemetry, analytics, or third-party user tracking scripts are included.
  • Auto-saved drafts and the sequential invoice counter are stored strictly in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and can be cleared at any time via the Reset button.
